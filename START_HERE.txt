SA MODKIT — play, do not compile
================================

You need:
  1. GTA San Andreas PC 1.0
  2. CLEO 4.4  (CLEO.asi next to gta_sa.exe)
     https://cleo.li

Then double-click:

  COPY_TO_GAME.bat

Pick the folder that contains gta_sa.exe. That is the whole install.

START.bat opens the same installer as a window. If a pack has a file
in prebuilt\, it is copied. Sanny is not used.

Sanny Builder is only for people who edit the .txt sources.
